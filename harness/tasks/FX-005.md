# FX-005 · Quests, points, and regional rankings

**Prerequisites.** FX-004 room membership, FX-006 circle accountability, and FX-007 checked practice outcomes.

**Goal.** Award points for eligible focus, practice, and squad quests, with personal scores and optional shared rankings. Read [game and rankings](../../docs/modules/M10-GAME-AND-RANKINGS.md) and [the scoring proposal](../../docs/SOCIAL-GAME-DESIGN.md).

**Scope.** Quest results, idempotent point ledger, friends board and opt-in region board. Use self-reported session outcomes and verified timing as scoring inputs. A squad challenge can count accountability completion. Keep private reasons, media, and peer praise out of scoring. Implement caps, duplicate/overlap handling, and rank visibility.

**Verify.** Run Q1–Q2 in the [matrix](../../docs/TEST-MATRIX.md). Check no duplicate award, weekly boundary, privacy after opt-out, minimum cohort and region aliases. Verify that past points are preserved when a student leaves or reports a missed goal.
