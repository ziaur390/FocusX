# FX-006 · Mutual accountability for larger circles

**Prerequisite.** FX-004 private rooms with a ready participant list and durable round membership.

**Goal.** Each ready student gives one peer response and gets one from another member. The current pair in FX-001 continues to work when more people join. Read [peer accountability](../../docs/modules/M11-STUDY-BUDDY.md) and [social game design](../../docs/SOCIAL-GAME-DESIGN.md).

**Scope.** Persist rotating assignments, own check-in versus peer response status, pending and missed actions, and the authorized fallback for an unavailable reviewer. Show a simple circle summary with a goal, outcome and response indicator per member. A person can leave and return to their pending result while their room access remains active.

**Verify.** Run P1–P3 and G1 in the [matrix](../../docs/TEST-MATRIX.md). Check 2-, 3-, and 4-person fixtures, stable assignment in a round, rotation across rounds, idempotent fallback, no self-review, and peer inability to edit a student's result.
