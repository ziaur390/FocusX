# FX-004 · Persistent circles and outside invitations

**Prerequisite.** FX-001 private room and FX-003 account profiles.

**Goal.** Let friends form persistent circles and invite someone from outside a circle to one room. Read [group sessions](../../docs/modules/M08-GROUP-SESSIONS.md).

**Scope.** Accepted friend relationships, private circles, room scheduling, subject and duration, invite preview, expiration, acceptance and revocation, and optional reactions. Expand beyond two participants while continuing to store separate ready goals and outcomes. Hand rotating review assignment and fallback behavior to FX-006.

**Verify.** Run G1–G3 in the [matrix](../../docs/TEST-MATRIX.md) with three or more accounts. Check that an outsider only gets the named room after accepting, the group sees member-only activity, and one departure does not end the session for the rest.
