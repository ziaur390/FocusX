# FX-008 · Optional private text and video updates

**Prerequisite.** FX-006 verified mutual accountability. The everyday result and peer response work independently of optional messages.

**Goal.** A student may share an optional text or short video after a round with selected accepted friends. Read [peer accountability](../../docs/modules/M11-STUDY-BUDDY.md) and [social game design](../../docs/SOCIAL-GAME-DESIGN.md).

**Scope.** Text, optional camera/microphone permission, preview and explicit send, authorized playback, delete/report and expiry. Start text first and add video later in the slice. Keep the proposed maximum at 30 seconds and expiry at seven days. Preserve an obvious skip path. Room outcomes and point awards do not require a message.

**Verify.** Run P4 in the [matrix](../../docs/TEST-MATRIX.md). Check camera refusal, skipping, selected recipient versus outsider playback, deletion and expiry, and completion of a normal accountability loop without a message.
