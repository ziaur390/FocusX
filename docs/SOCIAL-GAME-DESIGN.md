# FocusX social and game design

A private circle organizes study sessions. Each session combines a specific goal with a shared timer, a result, and a peer response. All ready participants take part in both sides of accountability, including the host.

## One accountability round

| Step | What each student does | What the room sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commit | Enter a measurable goal and mark ready | Goals and ready status by member |
| Study | Use the shared countdown and work on the goal | Time and participation status |
| Check in | Choose completed, partly completed, or missed; optionally add a short note | Your self-reported outcome |
| Respond | Acknowledge an assigned peer's outcome and optionally suggest a next step | Who has responded and what remains due |
| Review | View the summary and choose a next study goal | Goal, outcome, response status |

If Ayesha and Zia are in a room, each responds to the other. With three participants, Ayesha can respond to Zia, Zia to Sara, and Sara to Ayesha. Circle membership confers no permanent reviewer privilege. At each new round the system assigns each ready participant one other person, with everyone receiving one reviewer. For three or more participants, rotate the assignment offset across rounds and persist the assignment for the round. [Peer accountability module](modules/M11-STUDY-BUDDY.md)

A ready member can leave the session at any time and still report later. A goal can have partial or missed progress and an accountability loop that is complete. Open check-ins and responses remain visible for the agreed review window. When the window ends, show missed items explicitly. A reviewer can mark **No check-in received** for a peer who never posted, without attributing a study outcome to them. If a reviewer leaves or misses their response, allow another eligible room member to pick up the open review. Preserve both the original assignment and later responder. A peer's feedback never changes the original member's recorded outcome.

## Invitations and privacy

- Create circles for recurring friends, and invite accepted friends directly to a room. Share an expiring link or code with someone outside the circle. The preview shows host, subject, start time and duration. The invitee chooses whether to accept.
- Room access, friendship and circle membership are separate. Room goals, results and actions stay member-only. Personal practice mistakes and private notes stay private unless the student chooses to share.
- Use invite rate limits, decline and revoke options, mute/report tools, and privacy controls. Realtime updates follow room membership. Add opt-in outside discovery only after moderation and appropriate privacy controls are in place.

## Quests and points

| Event | Proposed points | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completed focus session | 1 per full 5 minutes | Up to 24 per day. Use the registered timer window and the participant's completed check-in. |
| Reviewed practice quest | 5 | Up to two eligible quests per day. Record one award per completion. |
| Weekly squad quest | 10 | Example: the circle finishes three accountability rounds across at least two days and the student completes their accountability actions in at least one. One award per student per week. |

A 25-minute completed study session and one practice quest earn 10 points under the proposed rules. Timer records and self-reported outcomes are inputs to eligibility. Use a server-side ledger and reject repeated or overlapping ranked events. Keep points already earned when someone leaves or misses a goal. Media, reasons for leaving, and peer praise do not affect scoring.

Personal scores stay private by default. Friends can participate in a shared board. The weekly regional board is opt-in, with an alias and a self-selected province or region. Use Asia/Karachi for periods, equal ranks for ties, and a minimum of ten eligible participants before showing a region. Participants can withdraw their ranking visibility.

## Private updates

A standard result and peer response complete the required accountability workflow. A student can choose to send a text or short video to selected accepted friends afterward. Show preview and recipients before sending. Start with text and add video in R7, with a 30-second clip limit, seven-day expiry, private playback, delete and report. Use neutral room status for people who leave or skip an update. [Video task](../harness/tasks/FX-008.md)
