# FocusX product design

The main action is **Start a study session with your circle**. Make the three personal actions visible throughout the session: **Set your goal**, **Check in**, and **Respond to a peer**.

## Core flow

1. A host opens a private room, chooses the subject and duration, and invites friends. Each person can accept or decline.
2. Each participant posts a concrete goal, such as “Solve 15 Physics MCQs and review three mistakes,” then taps **Ready**. The room displays everyone's goal.
3. When the host starts, participants see a shared countdown and the goals. Each person can leave while the others continue.
4. At the end, everyone posts **Completed**, **Partly completed**, or **Missed**, with a short progress note if they choose. A missing response stays visible as **Awaiting check-in**.
5. Every participant responds to someone else's result. In a two-person room, the pair reviews each other. In larger rooms the app rotates assignments across rounds. A response can acknowledge what was done and suggest a next step. If a peer still has no result after the check-in window, the reviewer can mark **No check-in received** and finish their own task.
6. The summary shows each person's goal, result, and peer response status. The next action offers a new session or a practical next study step.

## Screens

| Screen | Information | Main action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | Upcoming room, user's study goal, and due check-in or peer response | Start or join a room |
| Circles | Private friends/circles, invites, and upcoming sessions | Invite a friend |
| Ready room | Subject, duration, member goals and ready status | Set goal and ready up |
| Active room | Countdown, current participant goals, and simple reactions | Study together or leave |
| Check-in | Original goal, outcome choice, and optional short note | Share progress |
| Peer response | Assigned member's goal and result, with response options | Acknowledge or encourage |
| Circle summary | Who checked in, what outcome they recorded, and whose review is still due | Finish your response |
| Personal history | Your sessions and circle responsibilities | Review progress |
| Today's Study, later | A topic quest, checked practice and due review | Start a quest |
| Topic resources, later | Top recommended lecture or notes and alternatives | Watch or read |
| Rankings, later | Friends and opted-in area scores | View points |

Show **Partly completed** as a normal outcome. In an early departure, show a neutral **Left session** notice. Keep reasons optional and private unless a student explicitly shares them with chosen friends. Make the ordinary room result form quick enough to use without attachments. Keep the optional text or video action outside the required review steps.

## Visual identity

Use the FocusX dot and fine open-circle mark in a compact header. Keep most of the room white with Deep Ink text, a spacious timer and a simple member list. Use Electric Violet for the current primary action. Use Bricolage Grotesque for headings and Inter for goals, forms, and tabular countdown numbers. Use Fresh Mint for completed feedback with a clear label. [Brand identity](BRAND-IDENTITY.md)

Use visible keyboard focus, readable controls, straightforward labels, and restrained reactions during the timer. Keep the countdown, each member's goal, and the next accountability action readable on the mobile web layout.
