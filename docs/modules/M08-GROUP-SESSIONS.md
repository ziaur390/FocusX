# M08 · Circles and timed study rooms

**Owns** accepted friends, circle membership, private rooms, invitations, ready-up, reactions, and attendance. FX-001 proves the two-person room. FX-004 builds persistent circles, richer invites, and rounds with more than two people.

A host picks a subject and duration and sends a private invite. Friends and people outside the circle can preview and accept a room invitation. Grant friendship and circle membership separately. A preview may show subject, time, host and duration, while accepted membership gates room goals and live updates. Use expiring invitation tokens, scoped reads and writes, and revocation. Allow report and mute, and rate-limit invitation attempts.

Every ready participant posts an individual measurable goal before the host starts. Hold one shared deadline for everyone. A member can leave while the rest continue and share a result afterward within the review window. The host manages room scheduling and invites and has the same accountability obligations as other ready members. [Peer accountability module](M11-STUDY-BUDDY.md)

**Acceptance.** Two accounts complete a private shared round. An outsider cannot read goals or responses before acceptance. After accepting a one-room invite, the person can join that room with no broader circle access. Expired and revoked invites fail. One person can leave while the other continues.
