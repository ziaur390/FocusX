# Required FocusX features

| Required feature | Milestone | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private timed study room | R0 | Two invited students enter separate goals, ready up, and see a shared countdown. |
| Mutual accountability | R0 and R3 | All ready participants post an outcome and each has another member to acknowledge. Goals, check-ins, and completion of peer reviews are visible to the room. |
| Honest results and pending status | R0 and R3 | A student can report completed, partial, or missed. Silence is shown as pending or no check-in. A peer can acknowledge, encourage, or record **No check-in received** after the window without changing anyone else's result. |
| Leave whenever needed | R0 | One participant leaves or disconnects, the others continue, and the departing student can report their outcome later. |
| Accounts and history | R1 | Users can manage a profile, set a goal and see private history and outstanding room actions. |
| Circles and outside invitations | R2 | Friends can form a private circle. An outside invite previews and grants only the named room once accepted. |
| Reviewed practice and mistake revision | R4 | A student finishes one reviewed topic quest, gets explanations, and sees a next revision date. |
| Recommended study materials | R5 | The approved link with the highest eligible recommendation count appears first for that topic and filters. |
| Quests and scores | R6 | Focus, practice, and squad events have a visible, idempotent point breakdown. |
| Friends and regional rankings | R6 | A learner can see a friends board and choose to join or leave a province or region board with an alias. |
| Optional private text or video | R7 | Students can send a voluntary update to selected accepted friends. Privacy, deletion, and expiry work. A normal result and peer response are sufficient to finish accountability. |
| Timed mocks and pacing | R8 | A learner attempts a timed set, reviews the score and pace after submission, and receives a follow-up practice task. |

Peer feedback belongs to the session and its participants. A student's accountability completion means they submitted their own result and responded to the assigned peer or picked-up review. Completion of the study goal is tracked separately. [Feature list](FEATURES.md) · [Roadmap](PLAN.md) · [Test matrix](TEST-MATRIX.md)
